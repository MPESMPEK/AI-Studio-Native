# 🛡️ Master Anti-Plastic Negative Filter (Cegah Muka Boneka)

Sistem AI Studio Native secara otomatis menyuntikkan filter anti-plastik ke dalam `safe_neg` saat eksekusi:

```text
(doll, plastic skin, cgi, 3d, anime, illustration, fake face, blurry face:1.2), (deformed, distorted:1.3), bad anatomy, extra limbs, bad eyes, smooth skin filter, mannequin, airbrushed, cartoon, oversaturated
```

Filter ini secara efektif menekan probabilitas VAE menghaluskan tekstur kulit menjadi lilin atau boneka manekin.
